**Milestone Reference**
| Day | Milestone | Type | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Day 0** | **90 Day Deadline Notice** | Borrower Notice | 
| Notification issued to borrower that SAVE forbearance is ending and 
| 90-day plan-selection window has opened. | **Days 0-90** | **Plan 
| Selection Window** | Transition Period | CR 7495 90-day window. 
| Borrower must select a new repayment plan via the Repayment Calculator 
| at studentaid.gov, or be moved to a default plan. | **Day 90** | 
| **Move to New Plan Notice** | Servicer / System | If borrower has not 
| selected a plan: ED places borrower in Tiered Standard or Standard 
| Level. Notification issued, Tiered Standard is NOT PSLF-eligible. | 
| **~Day 120** | **1st Statement** | Servicer Notice | First billing 
| statement issued. Approximately 30 days after plan placement to allow 
| billing-cycle setup. Statement-to-due-date interval is ~21 days 
| minimum per federal regulation. | **~Day 145** | **1st Payment Due** | 
| Borrower Obligation | First post-transition payment due. Borrowers 
| without autopay must take active payment action. Missed payment starts 
| the delinquency clock. | **~Day 235** | **Credit Reporting** | Adverse 
| Reporting | 90 days past due. Delinquency reported to credit bureaus. 
| First adverse credit event for non-paying borrowers. | **~Day 505** | 
| **Default** | Terminal Outcome | 360 days past due. Loan transferred 
| to default servicer. Wage garnishment, tax refund offset, and 
| longer-term credit consequences become available collection 
| mechanisms. |
